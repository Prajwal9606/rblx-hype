In this town, every shadow turns solid the second the streetlights click on.

You see, bakers roll carts up shadow ramps. Kids bounce on mailbox shadows. And there's only one rule: never touch a giant shadow at night.

My name is Hype. My shadow is paper-thin and pale grey. Dax calls me Chalk.

Grandpa Otis always says, "A shadow is just a door with the light off."

Then the plaza power box blew. The giant clock-tower shadow slammed across Main Street like a steel gate. Dax charged it and bounced straight back. He was trapped by the broken switch.

That's when I understood. I turned my back to the last lamp and dropped low. My thin shadow slid 40 feet under the gate and flipped the breaker.

Clank!

The lights snapped gold.

Now Dax brings me flashlight batteries every Friday.
