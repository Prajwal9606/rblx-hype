In this town, every statue moves the second you blink.

You see, guards wear goggles. Shopkeepers take turns blinking. And there's only one rule: never blink in the plaza after dark.

My name is Hype. My eyes water in the cold, so I blink twice as fast as anyone. Dax calls me Winky.

Grandpa Otis always says, "Statues only chase what runs."

Then came midnight. The plaza lamps died. A giant stone statue stepped off its base and cornered Dax against the wall. Dax ran. It chased faster.

That's when I understood. I sprinted between them and froze dead still. I blinked hard.

Thud!

The statue stopped an inch from my face, bowed, and stepped back onto its base.

Now Dax brings me eye drops every winter.
